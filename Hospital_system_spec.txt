1. Introduction 
1.1 Purpose 
The Hospital Management System (HMS) is developed to automate and manage hospital 
operations efficiently. The system centralizes patient information, appointments, billing, 
pharmacy, laboratory services, and administrative tasks to improve healthcare service 
delivery. 
 
 
 

=== PAGE 2 ===
1.2 Scope 
The system provides: 
• Patient Registration 
• Doctor Management 
• Appointment Scheduling 
• Electronic Medical Records 
• Laboratory Management 
• Pharmacy Management 
• Billing and Payments 
• Inpatient and Outpatient Management 
• Staff Management 
• Reports and Analytics 
 
2. System Overview 
The system is used by: 
• Administrator 
• Doctors 
• Nurses 
• Receptionists 
• Laboratory Staff 
• Pharmacists 
• Accountants
